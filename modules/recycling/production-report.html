♻️ Recycling Production Report
Efficiency % = KG produced ÷ actual target KG × 100.  Actual target = target KG − output lost to downtime, where output lost to downtime = target KG × downtime minutes ÷ shift minutes (Morning 7 h, Afternoon 8 h, Night 9 h). Machine, shift and overall lines add up the KG and the actual targets first, then divide. Hover over an efficiency figure to see its actual target.
Downtime is taken only from the control room's stoppage log (control table): the stoppages of the same machine, branch, date and shift as each entry, for the machines linked in recycling_control_map. An entry never counts more than its shift length. Stoppages with no finish time are not counted yet, and an entry with no stoppage has no downtime.
