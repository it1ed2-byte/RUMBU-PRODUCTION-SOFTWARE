Flake LOTs (FLOT)

After production is completed, flakes are assigned to a FLOT. Export deliveries take bags from a FLOT — the remaining count of AA and AB bags is shown here.

Admins can edit or delete a FLOT from its card. A FLOT that has productions or export deliveries linked to it cannot be deleted; close it instead.
