How Stage 8 — Production works
  1. Production starts automatically when the plant manager sends an agent's material here (Stage 7). The start time is stamped for you — there is nothing to type.
  2. While production runs — over one day or several — the lab records each bagging session as a batch in Stage 9 (Lab Testing): its weights, Flake Scan result, bags and QR labels. They appear in the table below as the lab adds them.
  3. When the agent's material is finished (the Balance is about zero) and every batch is complete, press Complete Production. That records the end time, totals all the batches, works out the waste, and sends the run to Payment (10%).
