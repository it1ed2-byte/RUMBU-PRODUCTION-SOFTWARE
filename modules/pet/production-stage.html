How Stage 8 — Production works
  1. Production starts automatically when the plant manager sends an agent's material here (Stage 7). He also chooses the company the flake is made for, and can change it while the run is open.
  2. External material into production — if material from an external source (e.g. Kano Wash Plant, Lagos) was used, the assigned person records the source, LOT, and quantity.
  3. Batch entry: add a batch each time one is made: the grade (chosen from the active grades), the weight of each bag, and the supervisor(s) and shift leader ID (their names appear by themselves). Keep adding batches until the material is finished.
  4. The lab (Stage 9) tests each batch as you add it. Result approval: when the lab submits a result, the result approver sees it beside the company's limits, then passes the batch or asks for a retest or a rerun.
  5. Labels: once a batch is approved, the labels person generates, prints and downloads its QR labels.
  6. When every batch is approved and the material is finished, the batch-entry person presses Complete Production. Waste = Rumbu weight − total flake batched, distributed across categories proportionally.
