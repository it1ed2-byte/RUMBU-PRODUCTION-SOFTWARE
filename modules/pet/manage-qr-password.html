About Rumbu

A small "About Rumbu" QR code is printed on every bag label. Scanning it opens a public page with the company website, the email and the phone numbers you set here. Nobody needs a password for that page. The same phone numbers (the ones ticked Show) are printed in the footer of each new label.

Who can see this. Only the phone number of each contact ticked Show is public: anyone who scans the small QR code or reads a bag sees the numbers, nothing else. The office and the name are for your own reference and are never shown to the public. Untick Show to hide a number without deleting it. A contact with no phone number is never shown.
