How Stage 4 — Bottle Offloading works
  1. Pick a collection from your queue.
  2. Enter the Number of Bales offloaded and choose the LOT where the material is stored. The LOT is then marked occupied until Production is completed.
  3. Press Submit and confirm. If approvers are assigned to this stage, one of them reviews it next; if none are assigned, it moves straight on.
