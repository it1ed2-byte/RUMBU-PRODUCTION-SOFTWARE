How Company Standards work
  1. Add each company we produce flake for.
  2. Add its limits. A limit is a minimum, a maximum, or both, on one value from the Flake Scan result: the purity of a section, one material or colour inside a section, metal pieces, or sample weight.
  3. The plant manager chooses the company when he sends a collection to Production (Stage 7), and can change it later while the run is open.
  4. The result approver (Stage 8) sees the lab result next to the company's limits. Values outside a limit show red with the difference. The approver then passes or fails the batch.
Only a super admin or a Stage 7 Production Approver can manage company standards.
