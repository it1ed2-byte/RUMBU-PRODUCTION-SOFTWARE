How Stage 6 — Quality Control works
  1. Pick a collection from your queue.
  2. Choose the Bottle Grade, enter the average bale weight and volume (the bale density is worked out for you), and choose the percentage of each of the 8 contaminant types found.
  3. No waste weight is calculated here — it is worked out at the end of production from these percentages. If approvers are assigned to this stage, one of them reviews it next; if none are assigned, it moves straight on.
