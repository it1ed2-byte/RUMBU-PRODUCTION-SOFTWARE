How Stage 9 — Lab Testing works
  1. Open a production run from your queue. A run appears here as soon as the plant manager sends the material to Production (Stage 7).
  2. Each time the lab bags material — for example 5 bags on Monday, 3 bags on Tuesday — add a batch with that day's weights.
  3. For every batch, in this order: (a) upload that batch's Flake Scan result, (b) enter how many AA and AB bags, (c) generate the QR labels and print them. QR labels cannot be generated until that batch's Flake Scan result is uploaded.
  4. Keep adding batches until the agent's material is finished — the Balance tile shows how much is left. Then Production presses Complete Production in Stage 8, which records the end time and the waste calculation.
