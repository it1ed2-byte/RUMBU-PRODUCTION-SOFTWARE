How Stage 9 — Lab Testing works
  1. Production adds each batch in Stage 8 as soon as it is made: grade (AA or AB), the weight of every bag, the operator and the shift leader. It appears here straight away.
  2. For each batch: take the sample, test it, upload the Flake Scan result, then press Submit result to Production. Submitting marks that batch as tested, and you move to the next batch.
  3. Production then reviews your result and approves it, asks for a retest (you upload a new result for the same batch) or asks for a rerun (production makes the batch again). QR labels are released only after approval.
