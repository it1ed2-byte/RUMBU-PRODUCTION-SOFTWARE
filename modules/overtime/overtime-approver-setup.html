Overtime — Setup

Overtime Setup

Assign who does each step of the overtime workflow. Open a role to manage it on its own page.

Initiators — Step 1

Who may start an overtime request for a branch. Assign per branch; the same person can be added to more than one branch.

Currently assigned for this branch

Department Fillers — Step 2

Who may fill overtime for a department in a branch. Only their account can fill it, and whoever fills is recorded on the form.

Currently assigned for this branch / department

Staff Approval — Step 3

Give one person the whole branch, or a chosen group of departments in it. A branch can have several approvers, each over their own departments.

Currently assigned for this branch

HR Review — Step 4

Who reviews overtime for a branch. Only assigned people see this step, and it appears on their sidebar only.

Currently assigned for this branch

HR Payment Approval — Step 5

Central across all branches. Approves each overtime payment before it reaches accounting. Assign one or more people.

This is a single shared team. Anyone assigned here approves overtime from every branch.

Currently assigned (all branches)

Accounting — Step 6

Central across all branches. Processes the payment for each unit. Assign one or more accounting staff.

This is a single shared team. Anyone assigned here can process payment for overtime from every branch.

Currently assigned (all branches)

Overtime Archive

Central across all branches. Staff chosen here see an Archive button in Manage Overtime, and can archive an overtime that was started by mistake.

Archiving takes the whole overtime out of every step, so a new overtime can be initiated for the same date. Nothing is deleted: it is kept in the archive with the person's name and the reason they give, and only IT can bring it back. An overtime that has already been completed cannot be archived.
You do not have the right to add or remove these staff.

Currently allowed to archive (all branches)

Overtime Restore

Central across all branches. Staff chosen here can restore an archived overtime from the Overtime Archive page. IT can always restore.

Restoring puts the overtime back into the steps, each department at the step it was at. It is recorded with the person's name and the reason they give. Read-only Viewer Super Admins can never restore, even if listed here.
You do not have the right to add or remove these staff.

Currently allowed to restore (all branches)

Overtime Viewer — view only

People who can watch overtime: every request, the step it has reached, and the staff and hours. They cannot approve, reject or change anything. Pick the people first, then tick the branches they may view (or tick All branches).
