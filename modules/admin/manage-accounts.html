⚠ Use the Excel template: its Bank Account Number column is pre-formatted as Text, so leading zeros (e.g. 00023) are kept. Branches

The company's sites and offices. A person's branch decides which branch's data and lists they see. Which pages they may open is set in User Access, not here.

Departments and Units

Where staff actually work in each branch (for example Extruder or Weaving), and the units inside each department. Used by Staff Records, Overtime and HR.

Modules and Menu Groups

A module is a folder of pages in the sidebar (for example Overtime or PET Collection). It is not a department. Choose a module's group to move it in the sidebar. Who may open its pages is set in User Access.

A new module stays out of the sidebar until its pages are built and registered. A module with no pages is shown as not in use.

Change My Password

For your security, enter your current password before setting a new one.

User Access

Give or remove a login, set a person's role, branch and pages, change their username or reset their password. Staff without a login yet get one from Staff Records with Grant Access.

Choose a person on the left to see and change their pages, branches and login.

Account Activity Log

A record of changes to accounts and set-up: logins granted, roles and pages changed, passwords reset, branches and modules added. Filter it and print it as a PDF.

Choose My Menus

Hide the modules and pages you never use, so your sidebar and search show only what you work with. This changes only what you see: you can still open everything, and you can bring it all back at any time.
