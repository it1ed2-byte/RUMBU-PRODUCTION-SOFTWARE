Bulk Upload Staff

Every staff member in one upload goes into a single Branch and Department. Choose those first (Unit is optional and also applies to everyone), then download the Excel template, fill it in, and upload it. Required columns: Employee ID and Full Name. Optional: Rank, Email, Phone, Bank Name, Bank Account Number, Sort Code. Rows with a duplicate Employee ID (already in the system, or repeated in the file) are skipped and listed.

⚠ Use the Excel template: its Bank Account Number and Sort Code columns are pre-formatted as Text, so leading zeros (e.g. 00023) are kept. A plain CSV also works, but Excel or Google Sheets will strip leading zeros from a CSV unless you format those two columns as Text first. Account numbers that look altered are flagged after upload.

Add a Branch or Module

Modules are display folders for pages (e.g. "Overtime", "GatePass") — new pages still need to be built and registered separately. This is NOT the same as a real organizational Department (Extruder, Weaving, etc.) — manage those in "Manage Departments & Units" instead.

Manage Branches

Rename or delete a real Branch. This no longer gates which Modules an Admin or User can be given — that's set entirely in Manage Role now, independent of Branch.

Manage Departments & Units

Real organizational structure — where employees actually work (e.g. Extruder, Weaving), scoped per Branch. Used by Overtime and any future feature that needs to know a person's real Department.

Manage Modules
