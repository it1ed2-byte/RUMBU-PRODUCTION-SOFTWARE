🔑 Grant Access: Weaving Staff

One-time tool. Gives every Weaving employee a login. Their pages and branch are granted automatically.

What this does

  • Finds every employee whose department or unit is Weaving and who has no login yet.
  • Creates the login with username = Employee ID and temporary password 12345678. They are made to change it on first login, exactly like Grant Access.
  • Staff who already have a login are left alone. A login that already exists for an Employee ID is linked, and its password is not touched.
  • The database then gives each linked Weaving login its three Staff Evaluation pages and its branch, so nobody opens Manage Role.
  • Safe to run again: it only handles staff who are still without a login.
