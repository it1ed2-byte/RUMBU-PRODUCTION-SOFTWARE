Overtime — Setup

Overtime Setup

Assign who does each step of the overtime workflow. Open a role to manage it on its own page.

Initiators — Step 1

Who may start an overtime request for a branch. Assign per branch; the same person can be added to more than one branch.

Currently assigned for this branch

Department Fillers — Step 2

Who may fill overtime for a department in a branch. Only their account can fill it, and whoever fills is recorded on the form.

Currently assigned for this branch / department

Staff Approval — Step 3

Give one person the whole branch, or a chosen group of departments in it. A branch can have several approvers, each over their own departments.

Currently assigned for this branch

HR Review — Step 4

Who reviews overtime for a branch. Only assigned people see this step, and it appears on their sidebar only.

Currently assigned for this branch

HR Payment Approval — Step 5

Central across all branches. Approves each overtime payment before it reaches accounting. Assign one or more people.

This is a single shared team. Anyone assigned here approves overtime from every branch.

Currently assigned (all branches)

Accounting — Step 6

Central across all branches. Processes the payment for each unit. Assign one or more accounting staff.

This is a single shared team. Anyone assigned here can process payment for overtime from every branch.

Currently assigned (all branches)
